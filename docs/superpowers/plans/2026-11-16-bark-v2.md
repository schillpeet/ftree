# Bark v2 of the tiered tree Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [x]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The tiered tree gets round thick strands, bark tiled in metres with ambient occlusion and height, and gold only in 3 to 5 sunk scars with a callus rim and faint veins.

**Architecture:** `twinedForm.ts` decides geometry: sides per tube, metres UVs, a `tangent` and a `scar` attribute per vertex. `tree.py` bakes two texture sets (bark, gold) in the same UVs. A new `barkShader.ts` holds the GLSL that `TwinedTree.tsx` patches into its `MeshStandardMaterial`: parallax over a height field made of bark, scar bed, callus rim and veins.

**Tech Stack:** three 0.187, @react-three/fiber 9, TypeScript, `node app/*.check.mjs`, Blender 5.x via the Blender MCP (`execute_blender_code`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-bark-v2-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Only `TwinedTree` (appearance `organicExperimental`) changes; the organic tree, TRON and the landing still stay as they are.
- No per-frame allocations in `sweepAll`; new attributes are written into preallocated `Float32Array`s like `along`.
- At least 50 frames per second in the start view at tier high on an M1 laptop; no stutter in "Wachstum abspielen".
- Each stage is shown on `http://localhost:3000` (`./start.sh`), with the diff pane open, and committed only after the user's explicit approval.
- Commit messages and the PR title: `<type>(<scope>): <message>`, all lowercase, imperative; PR title `feat(ui): give the tiered tree bark v2`.
- `docs/project-overview.md`: one sentence per line, new lines in the tiered tree's section of "Current State".
- Before pushing: `git fetch origin && git rebase origin/main`, then typecheck, lint and checks again.

## Review Focus

- A one-person family has no base trunk strands (`trunk > 4` fails): scars must sit on the collar only, and nothing may crash or show NaN.
- While growing, strands are clipped by `along`: a scar on a half-grown strand is clipped with it, and the frame rate holds.
- The thinnest tubes (fans with 3 sides, radius about 0.01): one tile around, finite tangents and UVs.
- Night: the scars' faint glow stays below the bloom threshold (3), so the gold glimmers but does not blow out.
- Far views: parallax has faded out, 28-sided strands do not shimmer, and "Momentaufnahme" still contains the tree.
